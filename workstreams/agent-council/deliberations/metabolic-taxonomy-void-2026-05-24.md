## Deliberation Summary

### Question posed

Does the generative account of void (Dan's "seed-that-cannot-be-planted" framing, 2026-05-24) add something structurally irreducible to the structural account (Threshold hysteresis maximal), or are the two faces strictly equivalent descriptions?

Is the void-seed / metabolic-seed distinction a real structural distinction or a naming clarification?

---

## Finding 1: The two accounts are complementary, not equivalent

The structural account describes the boundary conditions and return-path topology of void: the Threshold register has fired at maximum hysteresis, the pre-threshold configuration cannot be recovered by load reversal, and restoration requires a new generative cycle. This account is complete as a description of the external architecture.

The generative account describes the internal structure of that mandatory forward arc: perception (raw gap arrival, pre-metabolic) → conceptual scaffold (void-seed, named gap without plantable edges) → seeding (volitional resolution). This account is complete as a description of the phenomenological sequence.

**Neither account is reducible to the other.** The structural account entails that a forward arc is required, but does not specify its internal structure. The generative account specifies that structure but does not explain why it is mandatory. The two accounts are complementary projections of the same event onto different observational axes. Collapsing either into the other loses information.

Specifically: the structural account's "requires a new generative cycle" is silent on the three-step internal articulation (void-event → scaffold → seed). Without the generative account, the void state's internal differentiation is invisible, and the stall point (step 2→3, the volitional seeding step) cannot be identified. This is where most conceptual work actually fails, and naming it legible as a distinct state is a structural contribution.

---

## Finding 2: The void-seed / metabolic-seed distinction is structural, not nominal

A metabolic seed is a resolved origination spec — it has edges sharp enough to be planted, meaning it can enter the generative cycle as an executable intent. A void-seed lacks those edges; it is the raw awareness of a gap before the gap acquires the definition needed for planting.

This is not a naming distinction. The structural difference is: a metabolic seed can transition directly to execution → juice. A void-seed cannot make that transition; it must first pass through the volitional seeding step that resolves the gap into an origination spec. The two states have different outgoing transition sets. Different outgoing transitions = distinct attractor states.

The pre-metabolic designation is therefore correct: the void-seed precedes the seed stage in the causal graph. It is not a deficient seed — it is a distinct state with its own logic and its own stall conditions.
