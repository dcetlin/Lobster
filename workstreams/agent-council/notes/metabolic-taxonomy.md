## metabolic-taxonomy

**Committed:** 2026-05-24
**Source:** Dan Cetlin — refined through council deliberation May 2026
**Zone:** systems-ecology

A closed generative system has eight distinct metabolic states (attractors), organized into in-process states, terminals, and threshold events. The taxonomy is structural, not metaphorical — each state has a specific causal role and specific transition conditions.

### States / Metabolic Attractors

**In-process states:**
- **Juice:** Generative potential in motion, not yet resolved. The undifferentiated energy of a system that has begun moving but has not committed to a form.
- **Transmutation:** Juice changing character through resistance registers. An in-process state, not a terminal — the resistance register (whether the load is generative or dissipative) determines which fork transmutation takes.
- **Seed:** Stable origination spec, ready for execution. A seed is not yet juice — it is a resolved form waiting to be activated. Can decay to heat if never executed.

**Terminal states:**
- **Pearl:** Stabilized insight. Becomes canon. A pearl is what transmutation produces when it holds under load.
- **Heat:** Dissipated energy, no residue. What transmutation produces when it breaks under load, and what seeds become when they decay without execution.

**Threshold / event states:**
- **Void:** A threshold recognition event with two faces. The structural face: the perceptual transition where a previously imperceptible thing crosses into conceptual scaffold visibility — the moment of first recognition. The generative face: a seed that cannot be planted — the raw arrival of juice processed into the revelation of a new gap, with the beginnings of articulation to identify it, with strong acknowledgement that something is here uncharted, but awareness that there IS something here. Void is not an absence — it is pre-metabolic: it precedes even the seed stage. (Dan Cetlin, 2026-05-24)
- **Shit (revealed):** Latent entropy surfaced by sweeps. The sweep is a sensor, not a metabolic engine — it surfaces shit that was already there, it does not produce it.
- **Shit (produced):** Entropy generated by execution itself. Conflict markers, broken migrations, bad decisions — the metabolic byproduct of work that goes wrong in execution.

### Structural Relationships

```
juice → transmutation → [holds under load] → pearl → canon
juice → transmutation → [breaks under load] → shit-produced → heat
juice → [sweeps] → shit-revealed → [addressed] → seed OR heat
juice → [recognition] → void-event → [named] → conceptual scaffold → [seeded] → seed
seed → execution → juice   [cycle closes — seeds re-generate juice]
pearl → canon              [terminal]
heat                       [terminal, no residue]
```

### Key Structural Notes

**The resistance register is the fork determinant.** Transmutation is not intrinsically generative or dissipative — the same energy in motion becomes pearl or shit-produced depending on whether the resistance it meets is in the load path of coherent emergence (generative) or outside it (dissipative). See: `generative-vs-dissipative-resistance` canon entry.

**Sweeps are sensors, not engines.** A sweep that surfaces latent entropy did not produce that entropy — it detected it. Conflating the sensor with the source is a systemic error: it produces misattribution (punishing the sweep mechanism) and obscures actual origin (where the entropy was actually introduced).

**Void-event is three distinct steps, not one.**
1. Perception (void): the thing becomes visible that was not before — the raw arrival of a new gap; pre-metabolic awareness before the gap has edges
2. Conceptual scaffold (the "seed-that-cannot-be-planted"): a concept or word attaches to the perception. This is the generative/phenomenological face Dan names: beginnings of articulation to identify, strong acknowledgement that something is here uncharted, but awareness that there IS something here. This step is the void-seed — distinct from metabolic "seeds" (which are resolved specs ready for execution). The void-seed is PRE-metabolic: it precedes even the seed stage, because the gap does not yet have edges sharp enough to be planted.
3. Seeding (volitional): the named thing becomes an origination spec ready for execution — the gap has been given enough edge to become a seed proper

Collapsing these three into "having an insight" loses the causal structure — specifically, it loses the volitional step, which is where most conceptual work stalls. Dan's framing makes step 2 legible as its own phenomenological moment: the "seed that cannot be planted" is not a deficient seed — it is a distinct state with its own logic. (Dan Cetlin, 2026-05-24)

**Seed → juice closes the generative cycle.** Seeds are not endpoints. A seed that executes produces juice — generative potential in motion again. The cycle is closed, not linear. Systems that treat seeds as final outputs accumulate seed-debt: potential energy that never re-enters the generative loop.

### Provenance

Refined through council deliberation May 2026. Earlier versions conflated transmutation with a terminal state and did not distinguish shit-revealed from shit-produced. The three-step void decomposition emerged from examining why conceptual work stalls after recognition: the stall is consistently at the seeding step, not the naming step. The cycle-closure property (seed → juice) was added to capture that execution is not consumption of a seed but transformation back into generative potential.

**Connections:**
- `generative-vs-dissipative-resistance`: the resistance register is the fork determinant for transmutation — that entry defines what makes resistance generative vs. dissipative
- `stiffness-toughness-tradeoff`: a material-science instantiation of the same underlying principle — systems tuned for resistance under static load fail under dynamic perturbation; transmutation toward pearl requires dynamic load-path coupling, not maximum rigidity

### Void state → post-Threshold liminal space

**Two-register convergence: structural and generative accounts name the same moment.**

The void state has two complementary accounts that describe the same moment from different sides:

**Structural account (Threshold hysteresis):** Void follows when the Threshold register fires: the old configuration has ended, the new one hasn't formed. Void's irreversibility is the metabolic expression of the Threshold register's hysteresis (resistance registers lexicon, Tier 2 → Hysteresis). The three-step void decomposition (void-event → scaffold → seed) describes the hysteresis arc: you cannot return to the pre-transformation configuration from within the same cycle. Restoration requires completing the generative cycle (scaffold → seed → juice) before a new Threshold-eligible state is reached. This is maximum Threshold hysteresis — the transformation was written permanently into the system (coupling the Threshold event to the Inscription register), so the return path cannot retrace the loading path.

**Generative account (seed-without-soil):** Void is also the generative face of the same moment — the arrival of juice processed into revelation of a new gap, with the beginnings of articulation to identify, with strong acknowledgement that something is here uncharted, but awareness that there IS something here. Dan's framing (2026-05-24): void is "a seed that cannot be planted." This is the phenomenological/generative face: pre-metabolic awareness of a new gap before the gap has edges. The "seed that cannot be planted" is the void-seed — distinct from metabolic seeds (resolved origination specs). The void-seed precedes even the seed stage; it is the raw awareness of a gap before the gap acquires the definition needed for planting.

**Structural = Threshold hysteresis maximal. Generative = seed-without-soil.** The two accounts converge on the same event.

Not all Threshold events produce void. Low-hysteresis Threshold events (e.g., tone onset at bow hair rosin threshold) allow return to sub-threshold behavior by load reversal alone. Void marks the specific case where Threshold hysteresis is maximal.
